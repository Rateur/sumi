---
title: Command bar
description: A timer, an app or a question, from one shortcut.
---

Press `Win+Alt+S` and the island becomes a bar. Type, check the chip on the right, then press Enter. Press the shortcut again or Esc to close it.

## What to type

- **A timer**: `25m`, `90s`, `1h30` or `timer 10m`. The bar folds into the timer.
- **An app**: its name as Start shows it, such as `Discord`, or `open` and the start of its name, such as `open disc`.
- **A question**: anything else goes to Claude. Name an assistant first to pick another: `chatgpt …`, `gemini …`, `perplexity …`, or `search …` for the search engine.

## The chip

The chip says what Enter will do, before you press it. It never guesses from your habits: the same line always gives the same chip.

- **Tab** turns it to the next target, **Shift+Tab** to the previous one: the first guess, Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, then the search engine. So `notes` opens Notes, and one Tab asks Claude about notes instead.
- **Esc** closes the bar and gives the focus back to the window you were in. A click elsewhere or switching apps closes it too.

## Where your question goes

Sumi doesn't answer. It opens the app when it allows it, otherwise your browser, with your question filled in, and you send it from there. Claude opens in its app when it's installed. ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and the search engine open in your browser, and the chip says so.

After Enter, the island shows what opens for a moment. If nothing can open, it says so.

## Privacy

Sumi sends nothing. The list of apps is read from Start and stays in memory. What you type is never saved, and the line is cleared when the bar closes. Your question only leaves your PC through the app or site you picked, after Enter.

## Limits

- One thing per line: a timer, an app or a question.
- An app installed in the last few minutes may only show up the next time you open the bar.
- While the island is hidden in fullscreen, the shortcut does nothing.
