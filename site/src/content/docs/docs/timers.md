---
title: Timers
description: Type a length in the command bar and the island counts it down.
---

Press `Win+Alt+S`, type a length, then press Enter. The bar folds into the timer. Timers count from the clock, so they stay right when the PC sleeps.

## What to type

- **A length**: `25m`, `90s`, `1h30`, `1.5h` or `2m30`. A number alone is minutes: `10` is 10 minutes. Up to 12 hours.
- **With a name**, before or after, three words at most: `10 min pasta`, `tea 3 min`, `timer for 10s`.
- **While a timer runs**: `+5` adds 5 minutes, `+30s` adds 30 seconds, `pause`, `resume`, `stop`. They act on the timer that ends first, or on the one you name: `stop pasta`.

French and English words both work: `10 min pâtes` and `minuteur de 10s` too. The chip shows what it understood before you press Enter. A time of day, such as `at 6pm`, isn't a timer: it goes to the assistant.

## On the island

- An amber ring empties on the left, and the time left counts down on the right.
- **Rest the cursor on it** to see its name.
- **Click it** for the panel: the name, the time left, when it ends, and two buttons, pause or resume and cancel. **Esc or a click elsewhere** closes it.
- Paused, the ring and the digits dim, and music takes the island back until you resume.
- Several timers can run. The island shows the one that ends first, and the panel says how many others run. Timers without a name are numbered.
- With music, the timer has the island and the music the bubble beside it. Click the bubble to swap them.

## When it ends

The island swells and shows the name, or "Time's up", with the time since the end counting up: `+0:12`. A few seconds later it folds into a small pill that keeps counting, `+3:10`, until you see it.

- **OK**, or a click on the pill, dismisses it. Typing `stop` in the bar does too.
- **+1 min** starts one more minute under the same name. On the pill, the buttons show when the cursor rests on it.
- Esc and a click elsewhere leave it there: you may not have seen it yet.

A timer that ends takes the island from music, never from a call. While Windows says you're busy, it still shows: it's an alarm you set.

## The sound

A short, soft chime every 10 seconds for one minute, then silence. It follows the Windows volume. It's on for now; Settings, in 0.10, will let you turn it off. The site's demo plays no sound.

In fullscreen, a game or a video keeps the screen: only the chime plays. When it ends, the island comes back down and shows the timer that ended.

## Limits

- No alarm at a time of day, no stopwatch and no pomodoro.
- The sound can't be turned off yet.
- Main screen only.
- A timer doesn't survive quitting Sumi or restarting Windows: nothing it shows is written to disk.
