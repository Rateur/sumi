---
title: Privacy
description: Sumi collects nothing.
---

Sumi has no account, no telemetry and no analytics. Version 0.5.0 doesn't use the network at all. What it shows comes from Windows and stays on your PC: the music's title, artist and artwork, and which apps use the microphone or the camera.

Features that need the network will stay off until you turn them on:

- **Weather** asks [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com) for the forecast of the city you enter.
- **Update checks** ask GitHub for the latest version.

What the island shows from your PC is never written to a log or a file.

The command bar opens the app or site you pick with your question; Sumi sends it nowhere, and the app list stays in memory.

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages, serves its own fonts and loads no tracker. On the home page, a question asked in the command bar demo is sent to Wikipedia, and only when you submit it.
