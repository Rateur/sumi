---
title: Privacy
description: Sumi collects nothing.
---

Sumi has no account, no telemetry and no analytics. Version 0.7.0 doesn't use the network at all. What it shows comes from Windows and stays on your PC: the music's title, artist and artwork, which apps use the microphone or the camera, and the battery of your PC and of your Bluetooth devices.

Features that need the network will stay off until you turn them on:

- **Weather** asks [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com) for the forecast of the city you enter.
- **Update checks** ask GitHub for the latest version.

What the island shows from your PC is never written to a log or a file. Battery levels are read when Windows says they changed, and are never kept.

The command bar opens the app or site you pick with your question; Sumi sends it nowhere, and the app list stays in memory.

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages, serves its own fonts and loads no tracker.
