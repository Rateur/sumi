---
title: Music
description: The island shows what's playing, in any player Windows can control.
---

When music plays, the island shows the artwork and an equalizer in its colour, and the time moves to a small bubble beside it. Sumi reads the music from Windows' media controls, so it works with Spotify, browsers, VLC and any player those controls can see.

## The bubble

- **Click the bubble** to bring the time back to the island. The artwork moves into the bubble. Click again for the music.
- Your choice holds until the music ends. A new song swells the bubble a little.
- With two players at once, they share the island and the bubble, and the time waits. Click the bubble to swap them.

## The panel

- **Click the island** for the title, the artist and the progress, with previous, play or pause, and next.
- **Drag the progress bar** to move through the song, in players that allow it.
- **Press Esc or click elsewhere** to close it.

A new song swells the island, unless Windows says you're busy.

## Pause

Paused music stays a minute, then the time comes back. Play it again and it returns.

## Limits

- A browser shows one player, the tab that played last, often without artwork.
- Some players don't let other apps move through the song. The bar then stays put.
- An app that closes takes its music with it. Spotify's close button may only hide it near the clock, so it keeps playing there.
- The title, the artist and the artwork stay on your PC.
