---
title: Fullscreen
description: The island hides during games, videos and slideshows.
---

When a game, a video or a slideshow fills your main screen, the island slides up off the screen and its window hides. When the app leaves fullscreen, the island drops back down. It never takes focus.

It hides when:

- a window covers your whole main screen without a frame, like a video in fullscreen or a game in borderless mode;
- Windows reports a game in exclusive fullscreen or a presentation.

A maximized window, Alt+Tab and Task View leave it in place. A click on another screen while a video plays keeps it hidden.

## Limits

- F11 and fullscreen video in a browser take up to a second to hide the island.
- A game in exclusive fullscreen hides it whichever screen it's on, because Windows reports it for the whole PC.
