---
title: Microphone and camera
description: A dot beside the island while an app uses your microphone or your camera.
---

While an app uses your microphone or your camera, a small black disc splits off beside the island, as on the iPhone. Its dot is orange for the microphone alone, and green while a camera is on, with or without the microphone. The island and its music never move for it.

## Which app

- **Rest the cursor on the disc** and it opens into a card. Each app shows with its icon, what it uses and for how long.
- **Move the cursor away** and the card closes. Esc and a click elsewhere close it too.
- **Click an app** to bring its window forward, even minimized or in the tray.

## Mute and camera

- **The microphone button** mutes, with Windows' own mute, the default microphones and every microphone an app is using. Unmute turns back on only the ones it muted, or all of them if you muted them in Windows. While it's muted, a crossed microphone stays in the disc, so you never forget it.
- **The camera button** brings the app that's filming forward, where its own camera button is. Windows can't turn the camera off for every app without admin rights.
- **The settings button** opens Windows' privacy page for the camera or the microphone.

The buttons follow what is in use: no mute while only the camera is on, unless the microphone is muted.

## Limits

- An app that captures sound or video outside Windows' audio and camera layers isn't seen.
- A microphone an app starts using after you muted isn't muted: the crossed microphone goes, and a click on mute takes it too.
- An app's own mute, in Discord or Teams for example, doesn't show: the crossed microphone is Windows' mute only.
- Which apps use the microphone or the camera stays on your PC.
