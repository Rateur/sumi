Sumi's icon sits in the notification area, by the clock. It shows what the island has, even while the island is tucked or hidden by a game.

## What it shows

- **An orange dot**: an app uses the microphone. **A green dot**: an app uses the camera. Rest the cursor on the icon to see which app.
- **Three bars**: music is playing. The tooltip gives the song.
- **The logo alone**: neither. The tooltip gives the command bar's shortcut.

## Click it

- **Left click** tucks the island, or brings it back. See [Tuck](../tuck/).
- **Right click** opens the menu: **Tuck the island** or **Bring the island back**, then **Quit Sumi**.

## Keep it in sight

Windows may hide the icon behind the arrow next to the clock. To keep it on the taskbar:

- **Windows 11**: open Settings > Personalization > Taskbar > Other system tray icons, and turn Sumi on.
- **Windows 10**: open Settings > Personalization > Taskbar > Select which icons appear on the taskbar, and turn Sumi on.
