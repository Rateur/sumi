---
title: Time
description: At rest, the island shows the time.
---

At rest, the island shows the time in your language's format: `21:05` in French, `9:05 PM` in US English. It refreshes once a minute and does nothing in between.

The island sits 8 px below the top edge of your main screen, centered. Clicks beside it reach the window underneath, and it never takes focus from the app you're using.

## Limits

- Main screen only. Choosing the screen comes with the settings, before 1.0.
- The format follows your Windows display language, not yet the clock format set in Windows.
